# Privacy Policy — 10 Seconds a Day

**Effective date:** November 16, 2026  
**Last updated:** November 16, 2026  
**Service:** 10 Seconds a Day Automation

## 1. Who we are

10 Seconds a Day ("we," "us," or "our") publishes original short-form educational content based on personal and family experiences with dermatitis and rheumatoid arthritis. Our application, **10 Seconds a Day Automation**, is an internal content-creation and publishing tool for our own authorized social media business accounts.

Our official PDF-guide page is: https://app.lava.top/en/products/495b0903-ed60-45bb-83a3-b8ce3d7aae87

This policy explains the information the automation accesses, processes, and retains. The app is not a medical service and does not collect medical histories from viewers.

## 2. Information the application processes

When an account owner authorizes a social network integration, the application may process only the information necessary for that integration, such as:

- Account identifiers, usernames, boards, Page IDs, channel IDs, and other publishing destinations.
- OAuth authorization tokens and related technical credentials granted by the account owner.
- Videos, images, captions, titles, descriptions, links, and posting schedules that we create or choose to publish.
- Identifiers and status messages returned by Pinterest and other connected publishing platforms, used to record publication results.
- Limited technical execution logs needed to maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the automation.

For the Pinterest integration, the intended access is to **our own authorized Pinterest account and boards** to create and manage Pins. We do not request access to unrelated Pinterest users' accounts, passwords, private messages, or browsing histories.

We do **not** use this application to collect viewers' health information, financial information, payment card details, or private medical records.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information above only to:

- Generate original educational videos and corresponding publication metadata.
- Publish authorized content to the connected Pinterest account and, when enabled, other owned social accounts.
- Save finished media and a record of publication outcomes.
- Maintain account authorization, prevent unauthorized access, diagnose failures, and comply with applicable platform requirements.

We do not sell Pinterest account information or use it for unrelated advertising profiling.

## 4. Services and data processors

Depending on which integrations are enabled, our automated publishing workflow may use:

- **Pinterest:** to publish Pins and manage our own account's authorized boards.
- **GitHub Actions:** to execute scheduled automation and store credentials securely as repository secrets.
- **Google Drive:** to archive completed media files, captions, and publication records in our controlled account.
- **Cloudinary or an equivalent media host:** temporarily to make selected media accessible by social networks that require a publicly reachable media URL.
- **Meta (Instagram, Facebook, Threads) and YouTube:** to distribute content on our authorized accounts.

Each third-party service processes information under its own privacy and security terms. We share only what is reasonably needed to complete the requested publishing or archival function.

Our PDF-guide sales page is hosted by **Lava.top**, which operates separately. Any purchase or payment data submitted there is handled by that service according to its policies, not by this automation.

## 5. Security and retention

Authorization credentials are intended to be stored as protected configuration secrets, not embedded in published videos or public source files. Access to the publishing workflow is restricted to the operators managing our accounts.

We retain account identifiers, authorized credentials, media archives, and publication records only for as long as needed to operate and maintain our own accounts, or as required by applicable law. Credentials may be revoked and archives may be deleted when no longer needed. Execution logs may be retained by the underlying infrastructure under its configured retention settings.

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your choices and requests

If you are an account owner who has authorized an integration, you can revoke its authorization through the relevant platform's account settings. You may also request information about access, correction, deletion, or other rights available under applicable privacy law by contacting us using the contact options on our official page.

We will assess requests in accordance with applicable law and the data we actually control. Information already made public on third-party networks may remain subject to those platforms' own removal procedures.

## 7. Children

This automation is operated for business publishing and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

## 8. External links and changes

Our content may link to Pinterest, other social platforms, and the Lava.top guide page. Their privacy policies apply independently.

We may update this policy when our integrations or handling practices change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with its updated date.
