Privacy Policy

For 10 Seconds a Day Automation  •  Effective November 16, 2026  •  Last updated November 16, 2026

This policy describes an internal publishing tool for our own authorized social accounts. We do not operate a medical service or collect viewers' medical histories.

1. Who we are

10 Seconds a Day ("we," "us," or "our") publishes original short-form educational content based on personal and family experiences with dermatitis and rheumatoid arthritis. 10 Seconds a Day Automation is an internal creation and publishing tool for our own authorized social-media business accounts.

Our official guide page is https://app.lava.top/en/products/495b0903-ed60-45bb-83a3-b8ce3d7aae87.

2. Information we process

When an account owner authorizes a social-network integration, the app may process information necessary to operate it:

  • Account identifiers, usernames, board IDs, Page IDs, channel IDs, and other publishing destinations.
  • OAuth access tokens and related technical authorization credentials.
  • Videos, images, titles, captions, descriptions, links, and publishing schedules.
  • Publication identifiers, statuses and limited technical execution logs.

For Pinterest, we intend to access only our own authorized account and boards to create and manage Pins. We do not request unrelated users' passwords, private messages or browsing histories. We do not use this application to collect viewers' health histories, payment card information, or medical records.

3. Purposes

We process information to generate original educational content, publish it to our authorized Pinterest and other social accounts, archive completed media and publication results, maintain authorized connections, troubleshoot technical issues, and protect the service against unauthorized access. We do not sell Pinterest account information or use it for unrelated advertising profiling.

4. Third-party services

Depending on enabled features, we may use Pinterest for Pin publishing; GitHub Actions to run scheduled workflows and hold encrypted configuration secrets; Google Drive to archive videos and captions; Cloudinary or an equivalent media host to serve media to publishing APIs; and Meta or YouTube to distribute content to our authorized accounts. Each provider processes data under its own terms. We share only what is needed for these operations.

The PDF-guide sales page is hosted separately by Lava.top. Payments and purchases made there are processed by that service under its own policies, not by this publishing automation.

5. Security and retention

Credentials are intended to be kept in protected configuration secrets rather than public source code. We restrict publishing access to operators managing our own accounts. Account information, media archives, and publication records are retained while reasonably needed for operation, or as required by applicable law; tokens may be revoked and archived files deleted when no longer needed. Infrastructure providers may retain technical logs according to their own settings. Absolute internet security cannot be guaranteed.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

Authorized account owners can revoke connections through the relevant platform's settings. You may request access, correction, deletion or other applicable rights concerning data we control using the contact method listed below. Requests are handled subject to applicable law. Public content published on third-party platforms may also be subject to those platforms' removal procedures.

7. Children

This internal business-publishing service is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through it.

8. External links and changes

Linked services, including Pinterest and Lava.top, have independent privacy policies. We may revise this policy to reflect changes to our features or processing and will update the date shown above.
