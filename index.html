My Experience

A long road to a simpler routine.

I lived with intense itching, inflammation, dryness, and painful skin for more than a decade. The experience affected my energy and everyday life, and I spent years investigating different approaches.

Eventually, I developed a short daily routine that I now follow. I wrote down the precise products, quantities, and steps for those who want to understand my personal experience.

“What I share is a personal observation — not proof that an approach will work for anyone else.”
Our Family's Story

My mother's experience with rheumatoid arthritis.

My mother has rheumatoid arthritis, a diagnosis repeatedly confirmed by doctors. At difficult points, everyday movement and simple tasks were painful.

Our family later noticed changes in her comfort and mobility. These observations matter to us, but they do not establish a cause-and-effect relationship with any routine.

Rheumatoid arthritis requires appropriate medical care. We do not recommend stopping or changing prescribed treatment.
